About Us

Learn more about our organization and leadership

Our Mission & Vision

Mission Statement

To provide strategic leadership and coordination in the management of human resources in the Borno State Civil Service, ensuring efficiency, effectiveness, and excellence in service delivery to the good people of Borno State.

Vision Statement

To build a world-class civil service that is responsive, accountable, and dedicated to the socio-economic development of Borno State.

Core Values

  • Integrity: Upholding honesty and transparency in all dealings
  • Excellence: Striving for the highest standards in service delivery
  • Accountability: Taking responsibility for actions and decisions
  • Innovation: Embracing modern approaches to service delivery
  • Teamwork: Collaborating for collective success

Ministries Under Supervision

The Office of the Head of Service coordinates and oversees the activities of all ministries in Borno State Civil Service, including but not limited to:

  • Ministry of Finance
  • Ministry of Education
  • Ministry of Health
  • Ministry of Agriculture
  • Ministry of Works
  • Ministry of Information
  • Ministry of Justice
  • Ministry of Women Affairs
  • Ministry of Youth & Sports
  • Ministry of Local Government
  • Ministry of Water Resources
  • Ministry of Environment
  • Ministry of Transport
  • Ministry of Commerce & Industry
  • Ministry of Budget & Planning

Key Departments

The Office comprises several specialized departments including Personnel Management, Training & Development, Career Planning, Salary Administration, and Records Management.

Parastatals & Agencies

We work closely with various parastatals and agencies under the Borno State Government to ensure coordinated service delivery and effective human resource management.

Our Partners

The Office of the Head of Service collaborates with various national and international organizations, including:

  • Office of the Head of Civil Service of the Federation (OHCSF)
  • National Institute for Legislative Studies
  • UNDP - Capacity Building Programs
  • World Bank - Public Sector Reform Initiatives
  • Various MDAs and Development Partners
